Learn Racing

A quick guide to reading the race card.

What is a scratch?

A horse withdrawn before the race. Mark it scratched so the remaining ratings are recalculated.

What is a furlong?

One furlong is one-eighth of a mile. Six furlongs is three-quarters of a mile.

What do win, place, and show mean?

Win means first, place means first or second, and show means first, second, or third.

What does sloppy mean?

A very wet dirt racing surface. Yielding describes a turf course with significant moisture.

What is Prime Power?

A Brisnet composite rating. Higher figures generally indicate stronger overall profiles, but no rating guarantees a result.

What does rating share mean?

A relative score among the current field. These experimental percentages are not calibrated winning probabilities.
